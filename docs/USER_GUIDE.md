# User guide

How to use TuitionHub as a tutor, coaching centre, guardian or student. Menu names below match the
sidebar in the app. Live site: https://tuitionhub-omega.vercel.app

> Email is not configured yet, so the live site cannot send password-reset or verification emails.

## Getting started (everyone)

1. Open the site and choose **Sign up**, then your role: Tutor, Parent / Guardian, Student or
   Coaching / Institute.
2. Enter your name, email and a password (8–72 characters) and accept the terms.
3. You land in your dashboard. Use **Log in** next time; **Sign out** is at the bottom of the sidebar.

Your session stays signed in across page reloads. If an administrator suspends an account, it is
signed out immediately.

## Private tutor

### Get verified

You cannot apply to tuition posts or appear in search until an administrator approves you.

1. Open **Verification**.
2. Fill in your education, subjects, classes and areas.
3. Upload a clear photo of your **National ID, passport or birth certificate** (required) and,
   optionally, your latest academic certificate or student ID. JPG, PNG or WebP; up to 4 MB each on
   the live site.
4. Click **Submit for review**. The details you submitted are locked while the review is pending.
5. You get a notification with the decision:
   - **Approved:** your profile becomes public and all features unlock.
   - **Corrections requested:** read the note, replace pictures or edit details, and resubmit.
   - **Rejected:** the reason is shown; the decision is final.

### Find and run tuitions

- **Profile:** add a photo, bio, fee, teaching modes and availability.
- **Find Tuition:** filter posts by subject, class, area and mode; save or apply with a note.
- **Messages:** accept chat requests from families and talk before agreeing anything.
- **My Students:** accept demo-class requests, propose terms (days, time, mode, location, fee,
  start date). When the family also confirms, the tuition becomes active and a classroom opens.
- **Classroom** (from My Students → Open classroom): schedule weekly classes, mark attendance, set
  homework and assignments, grade submissions with feedback, share materials and write the diary.
- **Payments:** record a month as due; confirm when the family marks it paid.
- **Reviews:** read and reply to reviews from families.

## Coaching centre

1. **Verification:** upload your trade licence (or registration certificate) and the owner's
   national ID, fill in the centre details and submit. Approval lists your centre publicly.
2. **Teachers**, **Profile** (branches and centre details), and **Batches → Courses / Branches**.
3. **Students:** admit students to your roster; optionally link their student and guardian accounts
   by email so they see results and fee reminders.
4. **Batches:** create a batch with subject, teacher, days, time, room, capacity and monthly fee,
   then enrol students. Each batch has its own classroom for classes, attendance and homework.
5. **Exams / Results:** create an exam, enter marks and publish (published marks are locked and
   students and guardians are notified).
6. **Payments:** generate the month's fees, mark payments, waive or send reminders.
7. **Reports:** students, batches, attendance, fees collected and exams published.

## Guardian / parent

1. **Children:** enter your child's student-account email to send a link request. Your child
   approves it from their **Profile**. Until then you cannot see their classes.
2. **Find Tutor:** search verified tutors and coaching centres; send a chat request.
3. **My Posts:** post a tuition need for a linked child; review applications, shortlist and accept.
   Accepting opens a conversation.
4. **Active Tuitions:** request a demo class, review the tutor's proposed terms and confirm. Contact
   details are shared only after both sides confirm.
5. Follow your child in **Schedule**, **Attendance**, **Homework & Assignments** and
   **Progress & Reports**. You are notified of absences, new homework and published results.
6. **Payments:** mark a month as paid (or dispute it). After a tuition starts you can review the tutor.

## Student

1. **Profile:** approve or refuse guardian link requests.
2. **My Tuition:** your classrooms appear when a tuition is confirmed or a coaching centre enrols you.
3. **Homework / Assignments:** read the instructions, add a note and attach a picture or PDF, then
   submit. You see the score and feedback once the teacher grades it.
4. **Schedule**, **Attendance** and **Progress:** classes, your attendance and grades, and published
   coaching results.
5. **Find Tutor:** search verified tutors. (Posting a tuition need as a student is available through
   the API but has no screen yet.)

## Safety

- Keep conversations inside TuitionHub until a tuition is confirmed.
- Use **Report** or the **Safety** page if someone behaves inappropriately; reports go to the
  administrators' queue.
- You can block a user from a conversation.
